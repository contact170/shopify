No — Cleo never stores your bank credentials. We use Plaid as a secure intermediary, which means your username and password never touch our servers. We only ever see read-only transaction data.
You can reach us directly through the app — just tap the help icon. You can also email us at team@meetcleo.com. Our support team typically responds within a few hours on weekdays.
